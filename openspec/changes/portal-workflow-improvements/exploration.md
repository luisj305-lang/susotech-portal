### Approaches

1. **Capability slices over existing authoritative records — recommended.** Add role-specific projections and narrowly scoped extensions for work types, attendance, provenance and export after their decision gates.
   - Pros: Retains all twelve requests, preserves financial/audit boundaries, supports independent verification and non-destructive rollback, and limits overlap with existing UI work.
   - Cons: Requires explicit cross-slice contracts; richer attendance or frozen statements could expand the model later.
   - Effort: High overall; individual queue/display slices are smaller than attendance/data/reporting slices.

2. **Unified workflow/attendance/statement model first.** Redesign shared read models and historical reporting together before changing the UI.
   - Pros: Could support immutable statements and broader reporting consistently if those needs are approved.
   - Cons: Larger migration, authorization and reconciliation surface; blocks simple usability improvements and risks inventing unrequested payroll/crew behavior.
   - Effort: High, with substantially greater migration and review risk.

### Risks

- **Semantic invention:** “revisados,” assignment event selection, amount/coworker meaning, color scope and catalog rules remain unapproved; implementing assumptions would change the request.
- **History/provenance corruption:** A global color-fallback change can recolor legacy drafts; regeneration date can misrepresent submission; current allocations can change an earlier week's report.
- **Authorization/accounting leakage:** Attendance must not imply responsibility or allocation. Coworker names do not justify exposing coworkers' money. Manual and normal income sources require deliberate access and aggregation rules.
- **Overlap and rollback:** Historical specs contain drift, shared UI is already redesigned, and multi-value/attendance data cannot be safely rolled back by deleting or flattening records.
- **Evidence/delivery readiness:** Mobile color is unreproduced; deployed migration state, device results, runtime totals and script validity are unverified. Three pre-existing dirty files remain protected: `src/lib/jobs/actions.ts`, `src/lib/jobs/geocoding.ts`, `src/lib/jobs/routing-actions.ts`.
